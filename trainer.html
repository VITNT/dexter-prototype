KSL uses a face-free 150-value schema: shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, and both 21-point hands. Legacy 225-value KSL recordings are converted automatically; old 30-frame clips are resampled to 60.

Accuracy safeguards
  • At least 5 raw recordings are required for every class; 20+ and multiple signers are strongly recommended.
  • Validation clips are never augmented. KSL requires a signer- or session-independent split and refuses recording-only validation.
  • KSL adds velocity, acceleration, hand-to-hand relations, and graph edges before the temporal network.
  • Two-hand grayscale crops are fused only when every uploaded KSL recording contains them.
